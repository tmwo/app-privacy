Frequently asked questions

How do I plan a walk?

In Plan, tap Plan a walk, name the route, and choose two places. Tap Save walk. Open the saved route to edit the stops, reverse their order, or open walking directions in Apple Maps.

Why can’t I check in?

Open a saved walk and tap Check in with photo at the stop. Take a photo and tap Save check-in. Enable Camera and Precise Location for Two Stops in Settings. The app needs an accurate fix within 150 meters of the stop. Try again from nearer the stop or outside with a clear view of the sky.

What if place search is unavailable?

Check your internet connection and try the place name or street address again. You can also choose Enter coordinates instead in the stop picker and enter latitude and longitude manually.

Where are my walks and photos?

Plan lists your saved walks. Journal lists walks with a check-in or field note. Open a walk to view its photos. Two Stops stores these records on your iPhone.

How do I delete a check-in or walk?

Open the walk and tap Remove visit at the stop to delete that visit and its check-in photo. Use the walk’s options menu and choose Delete walk to remove the route and all its associated records.
